Acrobot LQR: finite vs. infinite horizon

Both controllers use the same upright equilibrium, initial state, cost weights, and 0.1 s control interval. Explore the trajectories and feedback gains below. The finite-horizon controller is evaluated with a fixed look-ahead and reused at each step.

Short look-ahead (N=10)Produces much larger, oscillatory corrections for this initial state.
Long look-ahead (N=100)Its gain is nearly identical to the infinite-horizon solution.
What changesIncreasing the horizon changes the finite-horizon value estimate and therefore the feedback gain; at this model and sampling interval, convergence is rapid after the first few dozen steps.
